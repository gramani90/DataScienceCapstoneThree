MODEL SELECTION
-----------------------------------------------------------

The tuned Random Forest was selected as the final model
because it achieved the highest F1 Score and ROC-AUC of all
models evaluated. It also achieved the highest recall, correctly
identifying 68.3% of purchasing sessions.

Although the Baseline Random Forest achieved higher accuracy
and precision, the Tuned Random Forest provided a stronger
overall balance between precision and recall and was therefore
selected as the final model for interpretation and deployment.
